About Me

I am a fourth-year Nutrition and Food Science student at Menoufia University, currently studying AI & Data Science through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI).

My goal is to combine my background in Nutrition and Food Science with Data Science, AI, and healthcare to develop practical, patient-centered solutions.

I am particularly interested in the role of data in nutrition and healthcare. I want to learn how to collect, organize, and analyze data, identify patterns and trends, and use these insights to support better healthcare solutions.

My long-term vision is to become the founder of a healthcare or health-tech company that puts the patient first and connects healthcare professionals, nutrition specialists, data scientists, and AI within one integrated system.

For me, technology is a tool. The real goal is to help people and create meaningful impact.

Career Objective

To combine Nutrition and Food Science with AI, Data Science, and healthcare to develop practical, patient-centered solutions and eventually build a healthcare or health-tech company that connects healthcare professionals, nutrition specialists, data scientists, and AI within an integrated system.

Patient First

The patient should remain at the center of the healthcare system. A patient is not just a medical case, but a complete human being whose health can be influenced by psychological state, lifestyle, dietary habits, and everyday life.

"Don't be afraid. We are here to support you."

Future Healthcare Ecosystem

A comprehensive, patient-centered healthcare platform designed to connect professionals and organize relevant patient information within one integrated system.

Patient

The first priority. Supported with trust, safety, and continuous monitoring.

Doctor

Diagnoses, reviews data, monitors changes, and makes the final medical decisions.

Nutrition Specialist

Develops nutrition plans and understands the relationship between nutrition and health.

Pharmacist

Collaborates on medication-related services and supports medication availability.

Data Scientist

Collects, organizes, and analyzes patient data to provide data-driven insights (does not make medical decisions).

AI

Supports monitoring, identifies changes, and provides alerts. Does NOT diagnose or make medical decisions independently.
